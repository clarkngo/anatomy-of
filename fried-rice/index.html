Anatomy of Chinese fried rice

Five ingredients and five minutes. Most of the recipe is heat, timing and rice that was cooked yesterday.

The parts

Point at a part to find it in the figure; click it to step the cooking to it.

  1. 01

    Day-old rice

    Freshly cooked rice is wet and soft, so it clumps and steams. A night in the fridge dries the surface of each grain and firms the starch, so the grains stay separate and fry instead of turning to mush.

  2. 02

    Flame

    A restaurant wok burner puts out several times the heat of a home ring. The point is a pan hot enough that moisture on the rice flashes off at once, so every grain sears instead of simmering.

  3. 03

    Wok

    Thin carbon steel heats fast and changes temperature quickly. The round bottom sits in the flame, so the base is fiercely hot while the sloped sides stay cooler: food can be pushed up the wall to rest and brought back down to sear.

  4. 04

    Egg

    Beaten egg goes into the hot oil before the rice and is broken up while it is still soft. It sets into small curds and coats some of the grains.

  5. 05

    Soy and scallion

    Soy goes in late, around the hot wall of the wok, where it sizzles and caramelises before it reaches the rice. Scallion is added at the end so it stays bright.
